About 36 Labs

36 Labs is a seed-stage startup founded out of UCL’s machine learning department. We bootstrapped to 10 people, and now we're on the next leg of growth.

Our work focuses on improving how AI reasons about creative decisions. Models can now generate almost any form of media, but they still struggle to judge what captures human attention. To solve that, we’re building an engine that learns from how audiences respond to short-form video and uses that feedback to make better creative decisions. Videos made with our engine have generated more than 300 million views on TikTok.

The role

You will push the capabilities of what content our creative harness can make. You’ll be an expert on frontier video generation models, finding combinations of tools, workflows and trends that produce content optimized for social media. You will then turn those experiments into workflows that you ship and maintain in production.

You will work with the CTO and harness engineers on the systems our workflows use, and with Outer Circle Agency on creative direction for campaigns in music, film and consumer brand verticals.

To apply, you will need to submit one AI-generated video you made or a portfolio that includes your AI video work. Show us your taste and be prepared to walk us through your creative choices.

What you will do

  • Constantly test video, image and voice models and discover how to chain them into workflows that outperform what we use today
  • Identify winning workflows and ship them for our harness to run, understanding where they can fail and weighing up costs
  • Ideate and build new content styles that can be posted at scale, ensuring they are of high quality and have the formula for generating millions of views
  • Reverse-engineer existing viral formats and creator workflows from X, TikTok and Instagram into repeatable method
  • Work with Outer Circle on creative concepts and samples for new clients and campaigns, and use their feedback and live results to decide what to scale and what to cut.

What we’re looking for

  • Hands-on experience with generative media models or tools: Seedance, Wan, Kling, Omni, Higgsfield, fal, ComfyUI, etc. You have chained models together, not just prompted one.
  • Creative taste and understanding of short-form content. You have an intuition for what good content looks like and what will do well on social media. You can explain your choices around hooks, pacing, editing and sound.
  • You have shipped code and automated workflows. You can work with model APIs, chain tools together and write Python or TypeScript to get a workflow into production and keep it running.
  • You're extremely high agency and AI-pilled. You find a way to get things done without waiting to be told how. You move fast, you can go from "someone posted this workflow on X" to a working version within a few hours. Your mindset with any task is “how can I automate this using AI?”, and you follow the latest releases, techniques and recipes in the AI space.

Nice to have

  • You have ran a social media account and grown an audience, or executed campaigns on platforms like Whop
  • You have a background in video editing, motion design, film, photography or design
  • You have fine-tuning or LoRA training for image or video models
  • You have built evals or comparison tools for generative media.

Our stack

Python, TypeScript, Postgres, generative media and LLM APIs, and open-weight models.
